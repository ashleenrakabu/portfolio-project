About Forward Edge

Your trusted partner for countrywide transportation

Forward Edge is a leading transportation and logistics company with its main branch in Mombasa and a key branch in Nairobi. We move goods countrywide.

From the Port of Mombasa to any destination in Kenya, our modern fleet and experienced team deliver reliable, transparent and efficient transportation services.
