Soft Chapati

Layered, golden flatbreads that stay soft for hours. Perfect with stew, beans, or a cup of chai.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Rest 30 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Makes 8 chapatis

Ingredients

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 cup warm water, plus a splash
  • 4 tbsp vegetable oil, divided

Method

  1. Mix the flour, salt, and sugar in a large bowl.
  2. Add 2 tbsp oil and the warm water. Stir until a dough forms.
  3. Knead for 8 to 10 minutes until smooth and stretchy. Cover and rest for 30 minutes.
  4. Divide into 8 balls. Roll each into a thin circle, brush with oil, roll up like a rope, then coil into a snail shape.
  5. Rest the coils for 10 minutes, then roll each flat again into a circle.
  6. Cook on a hot dry pan for about 1 minute, flip, brush with oil, and cook until golden spots appear on both sides.
  7. Stack and cover with a cloth to keep them soft.

Tip: the coiling step in step 4 is what creates the soft layers. Don't skip it.
